Venue

Building

City

Leipzig is a city of more than 600,000 inhabitants in east-central Germany. It has a rich history and offers a wide range of opportunities for conference side activities, such as a historic city center, museums, and musical events, as well as many restaurants and clubs. It is well connected and easily reachable by train from all major German cities. It is located about 160 km south of Berlin, with the fast ICE train taking a little more than one hour. Leipzig has its own airport (LEJ), and major airports are only a few hours away by train: Berlin (<2h), Frankfurt (<4h), and Munich (<4h).
